## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Primary tone:** Clear, direct, practical — no fluff
- **Secondary tone:** Conversational, slightly opinionated
- **Avoid:** Corporate-speak, passive voice, vague promises
- **Style reference:** Feels like advice from a knowledgeable peer, not a brand

---

## Content Pillars

List 3–5 topics your brand consistently covers:

1. AI Automation — How to build practical AI workflows
2. Content Operations — Systems for producing content at scale
3. Marketing Systems — Measurement, distribution, and strategy
4. Build in Public — Behind-the-scenes of building a product or audience
5. Productivity — Tools and workflows that save real time

---

## Audience Pain Points

What problems does your audience face?

- "I produce content but don't know what's actually working."
- "I spend too much time on creation and not enough on strategy."
- "I want to use AI tools but don't know where to start."
- "My content calendar is always behind."

---

## Banned / Restricted Phrases

Phrases that must NOT appear in any generated content:

- game-changing
- revolutionary
- unlock your potential
- are you tired of
- mind-blowing
- you won't believe
- secret hack
- disrupting the industry

---

## Content Format Preferences

| Format | Primary Platform | Preferred Length |
|---|---|---|
| Short-form video | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts | 30–60 sec |
| Long-form video | YouTube | 8–15 min |
| Carousel | LinkedIn, Instagram | 5–10 slides |
| Article / Thread | LinkedIn, X | 300–800 words |

---

## Call-to-Action Templates

- "Save this for later — you'll want to come back to it."
- "Drop a comment if you want the template."
- "Follow for the next part in the series."
- "Link in bio to the full breakdown."

---

## Reusable Prompt Framework

Use this as a base instruction when generating scripts or captions:

```
You are a content strategist writing for [Brand Name].
Audience: [target audience].
Tone: practical, direct, no hype phrases.
Format: [Short video / Carousel / Article].
Topic: [insert topic].
Include: a clear hook in the first 2 seconds, 2-3 specific insight points, and a CTA.
Avoid: any phrase listed in the banned phrases section above.
```
